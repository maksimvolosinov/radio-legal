Privacy Policy

World Stations · Last updated: 27 October 2026

World Stations ("the app") is published by Atikin, Kastonu str. 6, Nemezis ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses. In short: we don't run accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't collect your personal data on our own servers.

Data stored only on your device

Your favorite stations, alarms, chosen skin and app settings are stored on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Radio stations and the station directory

Station lists and search come from the community directory Radio Browser. When you browse or search, your device contacts Radio Browser servers directly. When you play a station, your device connects to that station's own streaming server, and station logos load from the stations' websites. These services receive your IP address and the request, like any website; their own privacy policies apply.

Location (optional, Premium)

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location once to find which country you are in, so your country's stations are shown first. The location is turned into a country by your phone's built-in geocoding service (Apple or Google) and only the country code is kept on your device. You can withdraw permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. They process your payment and share with the app only whether your subscription is active. We do not receive your card details.

Notifications

Alarm and trial-reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. For questions, contact atikin.mb@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data use changes, and change the date above.
